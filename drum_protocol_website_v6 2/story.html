The Story of the Song

Drum Protocol • Mary M. Hanson

September 11, 2026

Dear Fellow Americans,

Drum Protocol is a national patriotic song written for the citizens of the United States of America. I composed the music in honor of my son, who is a world-class drummer. The lyrics were written to honor the veterans of the United States of America.

Drum Protocol was copyrighted at the turn of the century on December 29, 2000. It all came together around the time the Twin Towers at the New York Trade Center fell on September 11, 2001.

I expected the recording my friends and family made of Drum Protocol to be produced back then. But God said, “LET IT SIT.” So the song sat. And it did so for the next 25 years, 5 months, and 30 days.

When the Lord introduced the MP3 software to me, He then said, “PUBLISH IT!”

The track “DJ-HANSON (DRUM PROTOCOL)” on YouTube was released to the public on June 28, 2026, by artists M.A.D., right in time to celebrate America's 250th Fourth of July birthday celebration in 2026!

The Puritans and the early American Revolutionaries practiced the active Christian virtues of energy, force, and perseverance when standing up for our freedom, which we all still enjoy today. That helped America grow into a great nation.

If they could make our country great, we can use the same Christian principles to keep it that way.

Our American Founding Fathers believed in the Bible and studied it. They knew how to stand on the power and faith of the Word of God. They also knew how to pray. Since they did, we can too.

This short, quick prayer brings release every single time when said with a strong and grateful heart.

A Prayer for America

Our dear Heavenly Father,

I raise my hands to Heaven, O Lord, in honor of Your name, for power to stand against the evil, and strength to cure the pain.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

May God Bless America!

Thank-you.

Sincerely,

Mary M. Hanson
